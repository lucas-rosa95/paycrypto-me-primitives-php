# PIP-0003 — Public Address Derivation

Historical relationship: [paycrypto-me/paycrypto-me-primitives-php#3](https://github.com/paycrypto-me/paycrypto-me-primitives-php/issues/3) is the retrospective coordination and navigation anchor. PIP-0003 predates the Issue-origin rule; Issue #3 did not originate it. Its legacy identity is retained.

The [historical Execution Plan](EXECUTION-PLAN.md) records HOW and ORDER separately from this capability proposal. Its recovery does not establish approval or authorize execution under PIP Specification v1.0.

## Motivation

The first admitted Primitives requirement is to derive a Bitcoin receiving address from an extended public key without handling private material. This capability must be locally usable and keep protocol semantics within Primitives while delegating cryptographic machinery and standardized codecs. Early documentation, provider research, and delivery planning were recorded separately under PIP-0001, PIP-0002, and PIP-0003. This record makes the capability boundary and those historical contributions discoverable together without changing their identities.

## Materiality

The historical PIP concerns delivery of public address derivation. The [Primitives Canonical v1.8](../../canonicals/primitives/paycrypto-primitives-canonical-architecture-reference-v1.8.md) already accepts the public-key-only boundary, BIP32 public-derivation responsibility, separation from SLIP-132 policy interpretation, delegated low-level machinery, and the three initial Bitcoin address compositions. It is the authority for these decisions; this historical PIP does not independently amend them.

No change to an accepted governed decision is identified for the capability described below. Under the [PIP Specification v1.0](../../specifications/paycrypto-pip-specification-v1.0.md), implementing that accepted architecture or aligning these documents does not require a new PIP. PIP-0003 remains a historical proposal and work reference, not a newly admitted v1.0 proposal or approval to execute. No `PIP Decision = Approved` and exact approved-proposal revision are recorded for Issue #3; the old plan, reports, and work performed cannot supply them retroactively. A future material departure from the Canonical requires its own Issue and Materiality Gate assessment.

## Proposed change

Provide a locally usable Primitives capability that accepts an extended public key, a relative non-hardened public path, a validated Bitcoin network definition, and an address policy, then returns a receiving address or a defined failure. The complete initial capability covers P2PKH, P2SH-P2WPKH, and P2WPKH on mainnet and testnet. Compatible admission, derivation, and hashing behavior is shared; each address construction retains its distinct semantics.

Primitives owns the capability contract, protocol semantics, validation, failures, definitions, and composition. Qualified libraries or runtime facilities perform cryptographic algorithms, elliptic-curve mathematics, and standardized encoding and decoding. Provider-specific types and behavior remain behind Primitives-owned boundaries. This states the intended capability, not a selected PHP API, provider, or implementation sequence.

## Scope

The public-only flow includes extended-key admission, bounded public derivation, network and policy compatibility, address construction, and explicit provider selection and containment. BIP32 derivation semantics remain distinct from SLIP-132 version and address-policy interpretation. Network parameter variation belongs in validated definitions; the reference application's fixed paths, account-depth assumptions, and implicit policy do not become Primitives rules.

Seeds, mnemonics, private keys, hardened or private derivation, signing, transactions, address allocation, persistence, usage discovery, payment state, hosted integration, fixed-address validation, Taproot, Bech32m, Lightning, and other chains are outside this capability. Bitcoin Cash remains a reuse question, not a delivered feature. Core, SDK, Consumer, and external-project implementations are outside this repository's delivery boundary.

Acceptance of a material technical result remains subject to the Canonical's applicable Counter-Proofs, independent verification, and recoverable specification, vector, boundary, and differential evidence. The [Counter-Proof Protocol v1.5](../../protocols/primitives/COUNTER-PROOF-REPORT-v1.5.md) provides an optional standalone report format when repository-native evidence is insufficient. This historical PIP creates no additional verification or reporting rule.

It is research input, not a frozen PHP contract. The unresolved matters include invalid-child and effective-path semantics; key-version, network, and policy compatibility; input and result shape; path and definition validation; stable failure meanings; and qualified providers with independent evidence.
