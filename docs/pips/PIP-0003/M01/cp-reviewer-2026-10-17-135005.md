## Decision

**The bounded M01 deliverable is accepted as an independently reviewed contract draft.** No M01 correction is requested. This decision does not establish canonical compliance or production readiness for the public-address capability. M01 did not close CP-07, CP-10 or CP-11; later milestones record their own scoped decisions and evidence. The `FAIL` states below mean that the full Counter-Proof property cannot be established from M01's draft alone; they are not newly discovered defects or a demand to redo M01. The executor had already disclosed these gaps.

The [M01 exit criterion in the starting plan at `2aca94d`](https://github.com/paycrypto-me/paycrypto-me-primitives-php/blob/2aca94d8b0537c44d791744b8e74658e1b1342c8/docs/pips/PIP-0003/PIP-0003.md) asks for requirement IDs, an OWN/COMPOSE/DELEGATE map, vendor-independent contracts, qualification criteria, explicit open decisions and an executor CP assessment. The later [approved PIP revision](https://github.com/paycrypto-me/paycrypto-me-primitives-php/blob/72c4fa1f0a2c9449a93071b55bf20f85b0008621/docs/pips/PIP-0003/PIP-0003.md) and [Issue decision](https://github.com/paycrypto-me/paycrypto-me-primitives-php/issues/3#issuecomment-6102704555) are present-day governance context, not retroactive evidence that M01 had been approved when written.

The flow preserves common derivation before P2PKH, P2SH-P2WPKH and P2WPKH diverge; the responsibility map places ECC, hashes, checked/witness codecs and conversions behind a semantic boundary. Both checked-in SVGs parsed as XML. This is a source/structure check, not a fresh MCP render or visual approval.
- Verified that the relocated draft's local links resolve. No PHP runtime, provider, Composer package, address output or qualification suite was exercised for this documentation-only review.
